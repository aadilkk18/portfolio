About

AI and full-stack developer.

I am Aadil Kamal Khan. I graduated in Computer Science from IMSciences Peshawar in 2026 and I am from Chitral. I build AI products alone, from the database to the live deployment, under the name SpecOne.

The rule I keep coming back to

I do not ask a model to behave. I build the system so it cannot misbehave. Every project I have shipped makes the same choice in a different place.

  • Kairos. The language model explains. A quantitative engine does every calculation, so the numbers do not depend on what a model feels like saying.
  • LEO. The model never supplies Khowar. Every word comes from a database I taught it, because a model that invents words in a language I speak natively is worse than one that says nothing.
  • Mirror. The opponent commits to its guess before you pick, and a test checks that the screen looks identical whether it sealed 90% or 30%.
  • HireLens. The model rates six categories and plain code computes the final score.
  • Spinzar Gems. Records cannot be hard-deleted, and a stale edit is rejected instead of overwriting someone's work.

That is what reliable AI means to me: guarantees in code, not hopes in a prompt. I also write down what is not finished. The Kairos page lists its known gaps, because a project that says what it cannot do is easier to trust.

How I work

I am picky about my own code. Not other people's, mine. If something already works but I know how it should look, I will reopen it. So for me a system needs to be a 9, a 10, a 9 and a half but not a 6, not even an 8.

If something annoys me twice, I automate it. I learn fast and I adapt. Give me the problem and I will learn what it needs along the way. I pick the stack for the problem, not the other way round, and I test what I write.
